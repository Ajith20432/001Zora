💡 TIPS FOR SUCCESS:

1. START WITH GROQ + GEMINI
   - Groq is free and fast (ideal for Termux)
   - Gemini is free tier available
   - Get these two working first
   - Add OpenRouter, Mistral, HF for consensus strength

2. PAPER TEST FOR 2 WEEKS
   - Verify AI consensus quality
   - Check that partial TP executes correctly
   - Ensure intra-candle triggers aren't too sensitive
   - Monitor equity curve smoothness

3. TUNE VOLUME SPIKE THRESHOLD
   - Too low (1.5x) = too many false triggers
   - Too high (3.5x) = miss real opportunities
   - Start at 2.5x, adjust after 1 week observation

4. MONITOR DAILY CIRCUIT BREAKER
   - If hitting 4% limit frequently: reduce MAX_RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT to 1%
   - If never hitting: could increase to 5-6% (careful!)
   - 4% is balanced for most markets

5. WATCH FOR SLIPPAGE
   - Real fills will be worse than backtest
   - Paper mode assumes perfect fills
   - Expect 0.05-0.1% slippage on market orders
   - Add 20bps buffer to TP targets
